longDescription: |
  Every Sunday, the main road through Balat (Vodina Caddesi and the streets around the Çarşamba market area) fills up with a long, informal flea market. It's not a tourist market — the regulars are local families, university students, and the Balat creative-class crowd who work in the new cafés and antique shops. The vibe is relaxed and the prices are lower than Feriköy, with more chance of a happy find.

  What you'll find: secondhand clothes (vintage denim, 70s dresses, leather jackets), small electronics, old vinyl records, secondhand books in Turkish, hand-painted ceramics, vintage housewares, antique silver jewelry, and a sprinkling of old cameras and watches. The food stalls at the edges serve gözleme, midye dolma (stuffed mussels), and fresh-squeezed pomegranate juice — a few hundred meters of slow walking with snacks.

  Combine with the Iron Church (10 min walk downhill), the Fener Patriarchate (5 min uphill), and a tea at one of the new cafés on Merdivenli Yokuşu. Sundays get busy by 11:00 — come at 10:00 for the calmest browse, or after 14:00 when the families are eating lunch and the sellers start offering deals to clear stock.

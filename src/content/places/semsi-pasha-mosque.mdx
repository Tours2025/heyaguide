longDescription: |
  Şemsi Pasha Mosque is one of Mimar Sinan's most intimate buildings, completed in 1580 for Admiral Şemsi Pasha. The mosque is just 11 meters square at its core — small enough to feel like a pavilion rather than an imperial mosque. The trick is the location: it sits on a pier jutting out over the Bosphorus in Üsküdar, so worshippers inside could hear the water through the open windows during prayer.

  Outside the prayer hall is a stone terrace and a small garden with a marble fountain. People come here at sunset to watch the lights come on across the water in Beşiktaş, Kabataş, and the Bosphorus Bridge. The Yeni Valide Mosque is a 5-minute walk uphill — pair them. The Maiden's Tower (Kız Kulesi) is visible from the pier.

  This is one of those places that rewards a slow 20 minutes more than a quick photo. Order a tea from the kiosk by the pier and just sit with the view.

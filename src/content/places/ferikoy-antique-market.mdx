longDescription: |
  Feriköy Antika Pazarı runs every Sunday from about 09:00 to 18:00 (sellers start packing up around 16:00) on Feriköy Caddesi and the side streets around the Feriköy neighborhood in Şişli. It's not a tourist market — the regulars are collectors, dealers, and Turks in their 50s and 60s who have been coming for decades. The vibe is unhurried and friendly: a cup of tea in the plastic chairs at the corner çay bahçesi is the unofficial gathering spot.

  What you'll find: mid-century Turkish furniture, French and German clocks, brass samovars, antique silver flatware, old radios and gramophones, vintage cameras, hand-woven Anatolian kilims, old oil paintings, vintage vinyl, and the occasional surprise (a working 1930s typewriter, a stack of Ottoman-era stamps, a Bakelite telephone). Prices are negotiable but rarely cheap — these are dealers, not estate-clearers. If you're serious, come early; if you're browsing, come mid-morning.

  How to get there: take the M2 metro to Osmanbey or Şişli, then walk 10 minutes downhill into Feriköy. Or a taxi from Taksim in 10 minutes. Cafés and cheap kebab places line the main road if you want lunch afterwards.
